# How to improve the six project PDFs

## All of them
- Use one page format for every project (16:9 landscape like the AF7 book works best on screen) with a cover, a contents line and a closing page.
- Remove every "Lorem ipsum" and filler block before sharing.
- Give each palette swatch a visible name and HEX code, and add CMYK for anything that will be printed.
- Add clear-space and minimum-size rules for each logo, plus a "don't" page. AF7 already does this well.
- Export smaller files. The single-page Gilgit Baltistan PDF is 30 MB; downsample images to 150–200 dpi.

## AF7 Visual Identity Guidelines (45 pages)
- The strongest of the six. Structure, typography and spacing are consistent.
- Double-check the Pantone references on the colour page against a current Pantone guide before sending to a printer.
- Add print specifications (stock, finish) to the stationery and packaging pages.

## FANI Coffee (4 pages)
- "coffe" is misspelled in the Brand Positioning text and in the file name.
- The typography specimens contain random filler characters; replace with a clean alphabet and a sample sentence.
- The competitor logos (Gloria Jean's and others) are fine for an internal strategy page but should come out of a public portfolio piece.
- The pages are very dense. Split into 8–10 pages so the logo, uniforms and app each get room.

## 4K Brew (2 pages)
- Comic Sans MS is listed as a brand font. It works against the "premium" positioning; swap it for a warm handwritten or rounded face.
- Section numbers skip 08 and use 14 twice.
- The "Sea Anemone" swatch is the same colour as the page, so it is invisible. Give swatches an outline.
- The personality icons (diamond, leaves) look like clip-art from different sets. Redraw them in the line style of the leaf pattern.

## Discover Gilgit Baltistan (1 page)
- The logo sits on a busy aerial photo in the hero, which hurts legibility. Show it first on a flat colour.
- The neon-green hoodie, purple bottles and blue luggage are off-palette. Recolour the mockups to forest, olive and sand.
- Two tagline options are shown. Pick one for the final guide.
- The palette HEX labels are too small to read.

## AERO Hydrating Serum box (5 pages)
- Every page still has "Lorem ipsum" placeholder text (one is misspelled "llorem").
- Add a cover, the palette, the fonts and a short product story.
- The black AERO wordmark on the blue box has low contrast; try white.

## AERO Performance (1 long page)
- The red swatch is labelled "#0C0D37", which is a dark navy. The red actually used is close to #E73847.
- "BULT FOR HIGHER PERFORMANCE" is missing an "I" in the bottle section.
- The Detox bottle mockup shows another company's product (LiveGood). Replace it with an AERO-branded item.
- The typography block still has Lorem ipsum.
