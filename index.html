How a project runs

You approve each step before we move on.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We talk about your business and what you need, then agree the scope and price.

  2. 2

    Strategize

    I look at your market and competitors and set the direction.

  3. 3

    Design

    You see concepts on real mockups and we revise them together.

  4. 4

    Build

    Logo files, guidelines, print artwork and social and web files.

  5. 5

    Launch

    You get all final files, ready for the printer and developer. You own them.
