    How a project runs

    You see progress at every step, and nothing goes to print until you approve it.

    1. 1

      Brief

      We talk about your business, your audience and what the design has to do. I send back a short written brief to confirm.

    2. 2

      Concepts

      I research your market and present distinct design directions, each shown on real items like cards, signs and posts.

    3. 3

      Refine

      You pick a direction and we refine it together through focused rounds of feedback until it feels right.

    4. 4

      Deliver

      You get organised, print-ready and web-ready files, plus a simple guide so anyone can use the design correctly.
