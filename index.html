How a project runs

Five steps, with your sign-off at each one.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We talk about your business, audience and goals, and agree a written scope.

  2. 2

    Strategize

    Research, positioning and a clear creative direction before any drawing starts.

  3. 3

    Design

    Concepts shown on real items like packaging, signs and screens, then refined with you.

  4. 4

    Build

    The full system: logo files, guidelines, print artwork and digital assets.

  5. 5

    Launch

    Final files handed over, organised and ready for printers and developers. You own the result.

Hi, I'm Waseem.

I'm Muhammad Waseem Akram, a graphic designer and the founder of WA Creative Solution, a creative studio working from Rawalpindi and Cape Town with clients around the world.

I believe a brand should do a job. A logo has to be remembered, a package has to be picked up and a guideline has to be easy enough that people actually follow it. Every project here was designed with that job in mind, down to the dielines and the Pantone references.
