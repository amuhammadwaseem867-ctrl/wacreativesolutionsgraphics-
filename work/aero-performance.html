AERO Performance

A sharp, fast identity for a fitness brand: Built for Higher Performance.

Discipline
Brand identity, sports and fitness
Scope
Logo design, Logo guide, Colour guide, Typography guide, Merchandise
Type
Client brand project

Overview

AERO Performance is a fitness and sportswear brand. The identity is designed to feel fast and athletic on gym walls, kit and drinkware.

Challenge

Fitness brands live on merchandise. The mark needed to read from across a gym and stay bold when printed small on a bottle.

Solution

A slanted A built from a single stroke with a red speed swoosh, an extended italic AERO wordmark and the line Built for Higher Performance, in performance red, black, white and two greys. Dark and light logo versions cover every background.
