AERO Hydrating Serum

Carton, dieline and bottle label for a hydrating face serum.

Discipline
Packaging design, skincare
Scope
Packaging design, Dieline, Label design, Mockups
Type
Client brand project

Overview

Packaging for AERO's hydrating face serum: a folding carton, its print-ready dieline and a matching dropper-bottle label, all carrying the AERO wave globe.

Challenge

Skincare shelves are crowded with white boxes. The carton had to read as fresh and hydrating at a glance, and still fit every ingredient, benefit and barcode a retailer needs.

Solution

A single confident ocean blue with the wave-globe mark, clear benefit lines for deep hydration, natural glow and skin nourishment, and a dieline laid out with bleed and glue flaps for production.

Colour palette

  • Ocean blue#1574AA
  • Ink#131313
  • White#FFFFFF
