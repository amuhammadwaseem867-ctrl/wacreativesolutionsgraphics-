AF7 Packaging System

Cartons, poly bags and stickers for AF7 zippers, with print ready dielines. 23 pages.

Discipline
Packaging design, dielines
Scope
Carton design, Poly bag design, Sticker and label design, Dielines, Print specification
Type
Client brand project

Overview

After the AF7 identity, we designed the packaging the factory ships in: three kraft carton sizes, four clear poly bag sizes and two identification stickers, with a 23 page spec book for the printer.

Challenge

Packaging for an industrial supplier has to be cheap to print and quick to pack, and still look like the brand on a warehouse shelf.

Solution

Natural kraft cartons and clear LLD poly bags printed in one colour, AF7 Navy. Each carton panel has a set job: logo on the long sides, size and handling on one end, a sticker area on the other and the monogram pattern on the flaps. The same layout scales to every carton and bag size. The book includes the dielines, sticker templates and the print spec.
