AF7 Apparel Fastener

A complete visual identity and 45-page brand book for a Lahore zipper manufacturer, built on precision.

Discipline
Brand identity, guidelines, packaging
Scope
Logo design, Visual identity, Brand guidelines, Packaging, Workwear, Stationery, Web and app direction
Type
Client brand project

Overview

AF7 makes zippers and fastening components for garment makers and apparel brands. The identity is built around strength, clarity and quiet confidence, so the logo, colour, typography and graphic language work as one voice from a carton label to the company website.

Challenge

Industrial suppliers often look generic or dated. AF7 needed to feel as precise as the parts it makes and hold together across factory packaging, workwear, stationery and digital.

Solution

A heavy, compact AF7 wordmark with a strict construction grid and clear-space rules, a navy-led palette with steel and mist neutrals, Montserrat and Poppins for type, a zipper-tooth pattern and a line icon set. The guidelines cover packaging on three board types, polybags, workwear, stationery, the website and the app.
