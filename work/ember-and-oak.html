Ember & Oak

A luxury coffee brand built around slow craft, origin stories and an editorial online shop.

Discipline
Brand identity, packaging, web
Scope
Brand strategy, Logo system, Packaging design, Art direction, Website UI design
Type
Self-initiated concept project

Overview

Ember & Oak is a concept specialty-coffee brand shaped around carefully sourced beans and the rituals that make coffee memorable. Warm natural tones, refined typography and an editorial visual language make it feel timeless, tactile and quietly premium.

Challenge

Specialty coffee is crowded with near-identical kraft-paper looks. The brief was to signal craft and origin without clichés, and carry that feeling from a bag on the shelf to a product page on a phone.

Solution

A flexible mark and an origin-led packaging system where each bean, from Brazil, Colombia and Ethiopia, gets its own character inside one family. The website turns the physical identity into an editorial journey through story, packaging, craft and signature blends.
