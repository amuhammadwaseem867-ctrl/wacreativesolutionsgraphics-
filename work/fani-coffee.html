FANI Coffee

A warm, premium identity for Farhan Shafiq's coffee house: Brewing Good Vibes.

Discipline
Brand identity, coffee shop
Scope
Brand strategy, Logo system, Packaging, Uniforms, Signage, Social media, App UI
Type
Client brand project

Overview

FANI Coffee is a premium coffee house built on warm hospitality. The name comes from the idea of a fan: a person who appreciates, loves and supports. The brand positions FANI as a community of coffee lovers, not just a place to buy a cup.

Challenge

The local market already has strong names like Gloria Jean's and well-loved independents. FANI needed a recognisable, premium look that still felt friendly, and a system that could stretch from cups to uniforms, signage, social media and an ordering app.

Solution

A steaming-cup mark paired with a confident serif wordmark and the tagline Brewing Good Vibes, in espresso brown, caramel and cream. The system includes horizontal, stacked, emblem and reverse logos, uniforms, drinkware, takeaway packaging, storefront and wall signage, social templates and a mobile app.
