JenniBag Air Pump

Retail box packaging for a rechargeable mini air pump, in two black finishes.

Discipline
Packaging design
Scope
Packaging design, Dieline artwork, Product diagrams, Icon set, 3D mockups
Type
Client brand project

Overview

The JenniBag Air Pump Pro is a small USB-C pump for vacuum storage bags, inflatable beds, sofas and pool rings. It sells in retail and online, so the box has to show the product and its uses at a glance.

Challenge

A small, technical product with many uses and certification marks, all to fit on a compact tuck-end box without looking cluttered.

Solution

A matte black box with the pump shown at full size on the front, a clear Tiny and Fast headline, a parts diagram on the side panel and a Multiple Uses icon grid on the back. Design 1 uses a bold sans headline; the second design uses a lighter, more premium serif with a line-drawn product view.
