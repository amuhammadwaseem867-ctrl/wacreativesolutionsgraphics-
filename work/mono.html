Mono

A monochrome identity system built on typography, structure and restraint.

Discipline
Brand identity, art direction
Scope
Brand strategy, Logo design, Brand guidelines, Stationery, Packaging, Art direction
Type
Self-initiated concept project

Overview

Mono is a contemporary identity focused on simplicity, structure and recognition, designed to stay consistent across stationery, packaging, signage and social media.

Challenge

A minimal identity can easily become anonymous. The challenge was to make restraint recognisable without relying on colour or decoration.

Solution

A constructed mark on a precise grid, a black, white and red palette, typography that balances personality with clarity, and guidelines that run naturally from business cards to billboards.
