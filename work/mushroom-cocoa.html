Inner Elevate Mushroom Hot Cocoa

Stand-up pouch packaging for a sleep-support mushroom hot chocolate, in a dreamy night edition and a bold black edition.

Discipline
Packaging design
Scope
Packaging design, Dieline artwork, Illustration, Label copy layout, 3D mockups
Type
Client brand project

Overview

Inner Elevate's hot chocolate blends organic cacao with five adaptogenic mushrooms, magnesium and L-theanine to help people wind down at night. The pouch needed to sell that calm on a crowded shelf.

Challenge

Functional wellness products often look clinical. This one had to feel cosy and premium, carry a lot of label information, and still read clearly from the shelf.

Solution

Two directions on a 160 by 230 mm pouch. The night edition uses deep indigo, a moon, stars and an illustrated mushroom. The black edition uses a line-art cacao and mushroom ornament in copper with bold orange type. Both share a clear hierarchy, ingredient tags, icon badges and a full nutrition panel.
