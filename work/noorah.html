Noorah

A character-led lifestyle identity that turns a personality into a complete content system.

Discipline
Mascot, brand identity, content
Scope
Mascot design, Brand identity, Content system, Social templates, Merchandise
Type
Self-initiated concept project

Overview

Noorah is a playful mascot identity that gives a lifestyle brand a recognisable personality, visual voice and emotional connection across beauty, fashion and self-care content.

Challenge

Creator brands need to feel personal while staying consistent across hundreds of posts, thumbnails and products.

Solution

An expressive character mark, a soft but refined visual language, photography direction, social and YouTube templates, and merchandise: a system that lets the personality scale.
