Pure & Bold Beauty

31 page product catalogue in English and French for a Canadian skincare brand.

Discipline
Packaging, product catalogue, brand presentation
Scope
Catalogue design, Packaging presentation, Editorial layout, Bilingual typesetting, Art direction
Type
Client brand project

Overview

Pure & Bold Beauty makes body scrubs, body milks, oils and creams based on Moroccan hammam traditions. We designed the catalogue the brand sends to spas, salons and shops, in English and French.

Challenge

It needed to look as premium as the products, run page for page in two languages, and explain each product clearly for buyers.

Solution

Sand and cream pages with the red product photos, a thin serif for headings, a script accent and a repeating layout for each product. Every English spread has a French version. The book ends with formats, partner details and how the products are made.
