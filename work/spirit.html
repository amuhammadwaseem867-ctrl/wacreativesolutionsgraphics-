SPIRIT

A 53-page visual identity for an activewear and lifestyle brand, built on purposeful movement and quiet confidence.

Discipline
Brand identity, lifestyle activewear
Scope
Brand strategy, Logo and emblem, Brand guidelines, Apparel and merchandise, Packaging and hang tags, Campaign art direction, Website and app direction
Type
Client brand project

Overview

Spirit is a lifestyle wear brand for people who train, recover and live in motion. The guidelines set out how the brand is expressed across every touchpoint, with the logo, colour, typography, imagery and graphic expression working as one visual language across apparel, packaging, retail and digital.

Challenge

Activewear is crowded with loud logos and neon. Spirit needed to stand out by doing less: an identity calm enough for lifestyle wear and strong enough for performance kit, consistent from a hang tag to a phone screen.

Solution

An interlocking S emblem drawn from two continuous strokes, paired with a light, widely spaced SPIRIT wordmark. A monochrome palette of black, charcoal, graphite and white, set in Satoshi, keeps the product and photography in focus. The system covers logo construction, symmetry and minimum sizes, misuse rules, colour, pattern and typography, then applies it to apparel, bottles, yoga mats, bags, hang tags, packaging, a campaign, the website, an app and a smartwatch.
